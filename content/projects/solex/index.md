This VéloSoleX belonged to my great-grandfather. Its engine had not run for decades and was very dirty inside. In 2023 I took it apart and rebuilt it.

A Solex has its 49 cc two-stroke engine mounted over the front wheel, and it drives the tire directly through a roller.

{{ <fig file="engine-before.jpg" alt="The bare aluminium Solex engine casing, stamped Solex Moteur 49 cc, Made in France, on my lap before the rebuild" caption="The engine before the rebuild. Serial number blurred." /> }}

## What went into it

The parts list gives a good idea of the job:

- **Engine internals:** crankshaft, stator and small-end bearings, piston rings, crankcase oil seals, a full engine gasket set and new clutch linings.
- **Fuel and ignition:** the fuel pump diaphragm and its glass ball, fuel filters, an oil-bath condenser and a spark plug.
- **Intake and exhaust:** a new intake and exhaust manifold and a new exhaust pipe.
- **The rest of the moped:** tires, inner tubes, brake pads, the rear light, bulbs and a seat cover.
- **Cleaning:** a thorough cleaning, and the chrome parts polished by hand.
