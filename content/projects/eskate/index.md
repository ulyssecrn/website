In 2019, when I was 17, I turned a bamboo longboard into an electric skateboard, heavily inspired by the Boosted Board.

I built the battery myself: a 12S2P pack of 18650 lithium-ion cells, 44.4 V nominal, joined with a spot welder and protected by a BMS I sourced from AliExpress. It sits under the deck. A FOCBOX Unity drives the motor on the rear wheels.

{{ <fig file="bare.jpg" alt="The underside of the longboard before the enclosures, with the yellow 12S2P battery pack taped to the deck and a motor on the rear truck" caption="Before the enclosures: the battery pack and the motor." /> }}

The battery and the controller sit in two separate 3D-printed enclosures under the deck. Flat braided cables run across the top of the deck, under the grip tape, to connect them.
