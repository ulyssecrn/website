My first build, in 2017, when I was 15. A coffee table with an arcade inside: an old TV sits under the glass top, and a two-player control panel runs along one side, with a joystick and six buttons per player and four buttons in the middle.

I designed the table in SketchUp. A Raspberry Pi 3 runs RetroPie, and the arcade buttons and joysticks are wired directly to its GPIOs.
